About Me

I'm Amay Deep — a lifelong learner with a restless curiosity and a drive to grow. My path hasn't been linear, and I embrace that. I studied commerce but discovered myself pulled toward design, technology, writing, and problem-solving along the way.

Whether I'm teaching myself new design tools, tinkering with code, or crafting my thoughts into stories, I aim to stay adaptable and self-reliant. Finance and analysis are areas I'm especially keen to specialize in — they ground my broad skill set with focus and purpose.

More than anything, I believe in the power of change — that small, persistent effort to evolve every day. That's the spirit I carry into my work at The Bandar Co. : always learning, always moving forward.

What is the Bandar Co.?

The Bandar Co. is inspired by the spirit of the bandar — a monkey that sits in a grey area between worlds, playful yet restless, sacred yet often misunderstood. To me, it reflects the way I feel: someone moving freely between different paths, not bound by one definition, driven by a desire to explore and grow.

Originally, this started as an idea to build a space for students like me — a kind of ChillarParty — where chillar (change) symbolized those of us navigating the world in-between, not quite fitting into the mainstream. Over time, it became a personal venture that reflects my own journey toward redemption and self-realization.

I'm teaching myself design, dabbling in code, and crafting my writing — a jack of all trades slowly becoming a master of one. The Bandar Co. is a place where all those threads come together: a one-man company rooted in curiosity, resilience, and the freedom to evolve.
