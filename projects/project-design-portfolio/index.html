This collection features all the presentations, labels, and brochures I’ve created since 2023. The first two presentations were built using Pitch, while the rest of the designs were crafted in Canva.

For the Insignia 9.0 brochure and label backgrounds, I used Stable Diffusion 3.5 Medium alongside RealESRGAN x4 upscaling via ComfyUI to achieve the perfect royal blue, gold, and black theme. It took generating over 100–150 images before reaching the final versions — each upscaled image was around 30 MB before compression!

For Insignia 10.0, work was much easier because ChatGPT and Gemini had advanced enough and helped out a lot. Plus thanks to our event co-ordinators, work was much efficiently carried out. I'm really happy that I was able to implement feeatures from the Happening brochure, and to have set a bar so high for the next gen who will come after me.

Currently working on a stopmotion-animation channel/page particularly for insta where I will post emotional content I guess, focussed on a hybrid-story telling system using me and AI at it's core. Stay tuned, you may see an update in a week or two.
